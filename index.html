How to use GrantCompass
Tips for getting the most out of each feature
🔍
Find Grants
Select your focus area and state, then click Search grants. The eligibility screener appears first — answering it filters out grants you won't qualify for. Check all three sources (GrantWatch, Grants.gov, State portal) for the widest coverage. Results are ranked by match strength and show a success score estimating your competitiveness.
💡 Be specific in your mission statement — vague descriptions return generic results
💡 Grants.gov returns federal opportunities that rarely appear on GrantWatch
💡 Click Save to pipeline on any grant to track it in the Pipeline tab
🌐
Scan Website
Enter your organization's website URL and click Scan & find grants. The tool reads your homepage and About page to learn your mission, programs, and geography — then searches all three sources simultaneously. No form-filling needed. Best used when you want a cold start with no prior research.
💡 Works best when your website clearly describes your mission and service area
💡 The scan takes 3-5 minutes — the progress bar shows each step
💡 State portal results depend on your geography being detectable from the site
✍️
Review Writing — Overall Review
Paste your full proposal (or upload a PDF/TXT) and click Review my writing. You get an overall score, sub-scores for clarity, impact, and specificity, a before/after panel showing your weakest sentence rewritten, and six detailed feedback items. Enter the funder name for targeted advice.
💡 Aim for a score above 75 before submitting
💡 The before/after panel shows the single highest-impact change you can make
💡 Use Export PDF to share the review with your ED or board
📋
Review Writing — Section Feedback
Paste a single section of your proposal (need statement, goals, budget narrative, etc.) and select the section type. You get targeted feedback specific to what funders expect in that section, a stronger opening sentence, and key phrases to weave in. Use this iteratively on each section before doing the full review.
💡 Start with Statement of Need — it's the most frequently rejected section
💡 The key phrases panel shows funder vocabulary you should mirror
💡 Review each section separately, then paste the improved full proposal into Overall Review
📄
Review Writing — Funder Rewrite
Enter a funder name, paste their stated priorities (from their website or RFP), then paste your proposal. The tool rewrites your narrative in the funder's own language and shows every change it made. Use this when adapting one proposal for multiple funders — it saves hours of manual editing.
💡 Paste 3-5 sentences directly from the funder's guidelines as priorities
💡 Aim for 80%+ alignment score before submitting
💡 Download the rewrite as .txt to edit further in Word
💰
Tools — Budget Narrative
Enter your project name, list budget line items (one per line in “Item, $Amount” format), and a short project description. The tool writes a complete paragraph-by-paragraph budget narrative justifying each cost. Add the funder name for a more targeted narrative.
💡 Include indirect/overhead costs in your line items — they need the most justification
💡 Format items as “Program Coordinator (0.75 FTE), $38,250” for best results
💡 Download as .txt and paste directly into your application
✅
Tools — Checklist Builder
Enter a grant name and brief project description to generate a customized pre-submission checklist. Items are prioritized high/medium/low and organized by category (narrative, attachments, approvals, etc.). Check off items as you complete them — checkmarks persist in your session.
💡 Build the checklist at the start of writing, not the end
💡 Export as PDF and share with everyone working on the application
💡 High-priority items are typically required — missing any is a disqualifier
📅
Tools — Deadline Calendar
Shows all grants in your Pipeline that have deadlines set. Navigate forward and backward through months using the arrows. Color coding: red = due within 2 weeks or overdue, yellow = due within 30 days, green = plenty of time.
💡 Always set deadline dates when saving grants to the pipeline
💡 Use the Pipeline tab to update deadlines after saving
💡 Check the calendar weekly to stay ahead of upcoming submissions
📊
Pipeline
Your grant pipeline has four stages: Researching, Writing, Submitted, and Awarded. Save grants from search results using Save to pipeline, or add them manually with + Add grant. Click any card to edit details or add notes. Move grants between stages using the arrow buttons.
💡 Add internal notes to each grant (contacts, requirements, assigned writer)
💡 Pipeline data is saved in your browser — it persists across sessions on this device
💡 Use the Deadline Calendar to see all pipeline grants with dates at a glance
